My research lies at the intersection of geometric analysis, elliptic partial differential equations, conformal geometry, and mathematical relativity.

I am particularly interested in regularity questions for geometric PDEs, asymptotically Euclidean manifolds, conformal methods in general relativity, scalar and higher-order curvature equations, and the asymptotic analysis of isolated gravitational systems.
