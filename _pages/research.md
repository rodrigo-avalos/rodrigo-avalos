My research lies at the intersection of **geometric analysis, differential geometry, partial differential equations, and mathematical general relativity**. I am particularly interested in geometric PDEs arising in general relativity and conformal geometry, their regularity theory, and the geometry of asymptotically Euclidean manifolds.

My current research is centered around the following interconnected themes.

## Geometric Analysis and Conformal Geometry

I study geometric partial differential equations arising from conformal geometry, with particular emphasis on the **Yamabe problem**, conformal deformations of scalar curvature, and higher-order conformal invariants such as **Q-curvature**.

A recurring theme in my work is the study of these problems under **low regularity assumptions** on the underlying geometric structures. This includes regularity questions for conformal structures and conformal Green functions, as well as the analysis of Yamabe-type equations for metrics of Sobolev regularity.

## Mathematical General Relativity

A substantial part of my research concerns the mathematical analysis of the Einstein equations and the geometry of isolated gravitational systems.

I am particularly interested in the **Einstein constraint equations**, the geometry of initial data sets, and elliptic systems arising from their conformal formulation. My earlier work in this direction includes the thin-sandwich formulation of the constraint equations and Einstein-type elliptic systems.

## Asymptotically Euclidean Geometry and Isolated Systems

I study the geometry and asymptotic structure of **asymptotically Euclidean manifolds**, motivated in particular by isolated systems in general relativity.

One focus of this research is the relation between the regularity of conformal compactifications and the asymptotic behaviour of the original non-compact geometry. I am especially interested in geometric and analytic conditions governing asymptotic invariants such as the **ADM energy, momentum, center of mass, and angular momentum**, as well as geometrically distinguished coordinate systems at spatial infinity.

## Higher-Order Geometric PDEs

Another direction of my research concerns higher-order geometric equations, particularly those associated with **Q-curvature and fourth-order gravity**.

This includes positive energy and rigidity phenomena for fourth-order geometric theories and the study of Q-singular and related geometric structures on asymptotically Euclidean manifolds.

## Current Research

Current and ongoing projects include regularity problems for conformal structures on closed 3-manifolds, conformal Green functions and Yamabe metrics of Sobolev regularity, the extrinsic geometry of isolated systems at spatial infinity, Bach-flat metrics, and regularity questions for the coupled Einstein constraint equations.
